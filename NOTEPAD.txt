alerts, provides graphical analysis, detects unusual patient-count fluctuations, and 
sends reports to the DDHS.

1.Haar Cascade Classifier -- It is used to detect a face in the camera image.
2.Local Binary Patterns Histogram -- LBPH identifies whose face it is after Haar Cascade has detected the face.
